BO START/RESTART SERVICE FIX v2.0
================================

NGUYEN NHAN BAN CU KHONG CHAY
- Goi truoc chi co START-SERVICE.bat va RESTART-SERVICE.bat.
- Bi thieu thu muc "service" chua PowerShell va Node static server.
- Hai file BAT vi vay goi den file khong ton tai.

CACH CAI
1. Giai nen goi nay.
2. Chep TOAN BO 6 muc sau vao THU MUC GOC website:
   - START-SERVICE.bat
   - RESTART-SERVICE.bat
   - STOP-SERVICE.bat
   - KIEM-TRA-SERVICE.bat
   - HUONG-DAN.txt
   - thu muc service
3. Dung vi tri la noi co:
   - package.json
   - .env
   - dist
   - src

CAU TRUC DUNG
App Quan ly xe...
|-- START-SERVICE.bat
|-- RESTART-SERVICE.bat
|-- STOP-SERVICE.bat
|-- KIEM-TRA-SERVICE.bat
|-- package.json
|-- dist
`-- service
    |-- service-manager.ps1
    `-- static-server.mjs

SU DUNG
- START-SERVICE.bat:
  Khoi dong website. Neu da co dist thi khong build lai.

- RESTART-SERVICE.bat:
  Dung service, tu dong npm run build, sau do khoi dong lai.

- STOP-SERVICE.bat:
  Dung website.

- KIEM-TRA-SERVICE.bat:
  Xem website co chay hay khong, PID, URL LAN va log loi.

DIA CHI
- Tren may dang chay: http://localhost:8080
- May khac trong LAN: http://DIA_CHI_IP_MAY_CHU:8080

LOG
- .service\msg-car-web.log
- .service\msg-car-web-error.log

NEU BAO CONG 8080 DANG BI SU DUNG
- Chay KIEM-TRA-SERVICE.bat de xem PID.
- Mo CMD quyen Administrator va dung:
  taskkill /PID SO_PID /F
- Sau do chay lai START-SERVICE.bat.

FIREWALL CHO MAY KHAC TRUY CAP
Mo PowerShell quyen Administrator:
New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "MSG Car Web 8080" -Direction Inbound -Protocol TCP -LocalPort 8080 -Action Allow
