BAN CAP NHAT v2.4.0

NOI DUNG CHINH
- Nang cap giao dien Tai xe mobile.
- Bat buoc kiem tra HTTPS, GPS va Thong bao truoc khi nhan/thuc hien chuyen.
- Sua luong lay GPS va thong bao loi chi tiet hon.
- Them thong bao uu tien khi tai xe co chuyen moi.
- Them server HTTPS cong 8443.
- Them script TAO-HTTPS-NOI-BO.bat.

CAP NHAT
1. Chep de source moi, giu lai file .env cu.
2. Cai mkcert tren may chu Windows.
3. Chay TAO-HTTPS-NOI-BO.bat bang quyen Administrator.
4. Chep .certs\CA-GOC-MKCERT.crt sang dien thoai va cai CA.
5. Mo https://IP-MAY-CHU:8443 tren dien thoai.
6. Bat Vi tri va Thong bao trong man hinh thiet lap bat buoc.
7. Go PWA HTTP cu va cai lai PWA tu dia chi HTTPS.
